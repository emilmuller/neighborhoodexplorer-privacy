Neighborhood Explorer privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Neighborhood Explorer is made by Emil Müller. The short version: the app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and your walks and your home location stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your home area, the walks you record (GPS tracks, times and distances), the streets you have walked, names you give your walks and your settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any server, and we never receive this data.

Location

The app uses your precise location to record walks, to give turn-by-turn directions and to plan a route from where you are. While a walk is recording, it keeps using location in the background with a visible notification, so it works with the screen off. Location is not used when you are not on a walk or planning one, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Health Connect

If you choose to import past walks, the app reads your walking, running, hiking and wheelchair exercise sessions and their routes from Health Connect. It uses them only to mark the streets you have walked. This data stays on your phone and is never shared or used for advertising.

Services the app talks to

  • OpenStreetMap servers (Overpass API): to download the streets and places (like playgrounds or cafés) around your chosen area, the app sends a map position and radius. No account, identifier or walk data is sent.
  • Terrain heights (Mapzen terrain tiles on Amazon Web Services open data): to know how steep streets are, the app downloads terrain tiles covering your chosen area, the same way map images are loaded. Only tile coordinates are requested.
  • OpenFreeMap: map images are loaded from OpenFreeMap as you look at the map, like any online map.
  • Place search: when you search for a place by name, the text you type is sent to your phone's built-in geocoder or to Photon (photon.komoot.io), an OpenStreetMap search service.
  • Google Play: purchases and in-app review prompts are handled by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details.

These services see your device's IP address when the app contacts them, as with any internet request.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's walks and settings in your personal device backup, so they come back on a new phone. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we cannot access it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share or rent any data. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a walk from its details screen, reset your map in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it has stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
